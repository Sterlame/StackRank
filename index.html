What bracket means here

A starting estimate, not a ranking
Bracket is a self-reported power level — 1 (casual) up to 5 (cEDH), with 3.5 as the tier between mid and high power. It's just where a deck's pilot thinks it sits before any games are logged.
The leaderboard doesn't rank by bracket. It ranks by win rate and how fast a deck closes out games (average turn of its wins), once real games are on the board. A high bracket with a low win rate and slow wins isn't actually competitive — the point is to let the data show where a deck really lands, with bracket as just the initial guess.
